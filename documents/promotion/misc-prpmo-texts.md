Although Plantour can be used for planning, preparing, and executing trips for individual travelers, its primary value lies in organizing trips and servicing groups. 

A travel agency employee can act as a trip administrator, using AI to create itineraries and activities, invite participants (participants use Plantour for free), and assign responsibility for shared items, tasks, and expenses. Participants can also exchange trip-related messages and create individual packing lists (including AI-suggested) as well as lists of expenses, tasks, activities and notes with both text and images.

Plantour  eliminates the stress of fragmented travel by consolidating scattered itineraries, group logistics, and AI-driven preparation into a single source of truth
