Hello and welcome to Plantur.
This video explains how to get started with Plantour.

Plantur offers the opportunity to explore its features without registration, using specially prefilled test data. To do this, go to the home page https://plantour.app, click the "Open a sample trip" button and click Yes. Now you are in Guest Access Mode as Robin Miles for 14 days. The app works with all functions, except for restrictions on the number of items, todos, travelers, and some other entities. To get started, add items to your current trip "Weekend in Las Vegas", pack them into bags, and download a packing list. If you need help, please follow the link "How to take the first steps." Good luck!
