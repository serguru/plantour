Hello and welcome to Plantur.
This video explains how to sign in or sign up on Plantour.


Getting started with Plantour is easy. First, it’s important to choose the right account type: **Admin** or **Participant**.

**If you’re the trip organizer**, you’ll sign in as an **Admin**. Head to the toolbar and select Sign In. You can jump right in using your Google or Facebook account, or simply enter your email to receive a secure sign-in link. If it’s your first time, we’ll automatically create your account. Once you’re in, you can start building trips, managing travelers, and inviting others to join the plan.

**If you’ve been invited to a trip**, you’ll sign in as a **Participant**. Check your inbox for an invitation email from your Admin. It contains a direct link to sign in, or a unique access code. Just click the link, or switch the sign-in form to "Participant" and enter your code. You’ll immediately see the trips you’ve been added to, where you can view details and collaborate.

Whether you're leading the way or joining the journey, Plantour makes trip planning simple. Sign in and let’s get moving!
