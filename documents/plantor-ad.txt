Plantour is a web app that helps individuals or groups pack for a trip. The following features are supported:
- The app is fully optimized for mobile devices
- Packing List
- Populate a packing list from pre-populated templates
- Ask the AI ​​a question, such as, "I'm going on a week-long safari to Africa in a month. What items should I pack?" and get a list of recommended items
- Travel List
- Trip List
- Users can be admins or members
- Members can create lists of items and packages they own, then copy them individually or in groups to a trip packing list
- Admins can do everything members can do, plus create trips and invite members
- Trip templates are supported to quickly create a ready-made packing list for trips like "Fishing Trip," "Weekend Camping Trip," or "10-Day Ocean Cruise"
- Quick answers to the question: "Which package is my blue tie in?" - Automatic creation and printing of packing slips
- Only administrators need to register to use the app; participants invited by administrators can access the app for free without registration.
- Ability to test the app without registration, using pre-prepared test data.
- Ability to create a list of shared items for a group of travelers and assign a person responsible for each package or item.
- Quick account creation or login via social media or email is supported; passwords are not used.
