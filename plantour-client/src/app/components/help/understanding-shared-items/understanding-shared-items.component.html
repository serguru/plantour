Understanding Shared Items

Shared Items allow trip admins to create collaborative packing tasks and assign them to participants. This helps coordinate who is responsible for packing specific items during the trip.

What Are Shared Items?

Shared Items are packing items created by trip admins specifically for collaborative trip planning. They differ from regular items in that they are assigned to individual participants with clear responsibility tracking. Participants must accept assignments and mark items as finished when packed.
