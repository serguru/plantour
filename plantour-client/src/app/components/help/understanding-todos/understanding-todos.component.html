Understanding Todos

Todos are reusable action reminders. Use them for tasks like booking confirmations, paperwork, charging devices, or anything that should be done before or during a trip but is not itself a packable item.

What Are Dictionary Todos?

Dictionary todos are your reusable master list. They work like dictionary items: you maintain them once, then copy the relevant ones into trips when needed.
