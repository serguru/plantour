Remember: Rejecting an assignment is perfectly acceptable. It's much better to be honest about your capacity than to overcommit and disappoint your teammates.
Pro Tip: If you provide a reason and a suggestion for who else might handle the item, it makes it much easier for the admin to reassign quickly.
