Understanding Comments

Trip Comments allow you and your participants to collaborate and communicate within the trip itself. It's a central place to discuss logistics, coordinate plans, and keep everyone informed.

What Are Trip Comments?

Trip Comments are messages that all trip participants can see and respond to. Unlike private messages, comments are visible to everyone, ensuring that important information is shared transparently. They are perfect for discussing trip-related topics and making quick decisions as a group.
