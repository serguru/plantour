Understanding Trip Bags

Trip Bags allow you to link your existing bags to specific trips and assign them to travelers. This helps you track luggage for each journey and coordinate packing among trip participants.

What Are Trip Bags?

Trip Bags represent your physical bags (luggage, backpacks, carry-ons) in the context of a specific trip. When you add a bag to a trip, you create a "trip bag" that can be assigned to a traveler and used to organize items for that journey.
